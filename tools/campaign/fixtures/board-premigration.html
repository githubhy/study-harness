No second campaign yet

A new one starts by creating campaigns/<subject-slug>/ with a CONTEXT.md and a roadmap.html, then adding a row to CONTEXT-MAP.md. Copy the roadmap's structure, not its content — the thread menu is filed under the agent loop's five steps, and a different subject needs a different spine.

What a campaign owns, and what it borrows

The method outlives any one campaign, so it lives at the repo root. Anything that would be re-derived for a different subject lives inside the campaign.

Shared language

Method vocabulary — the same in every campaign, so defined once in CONTEXT-MAP.md rather than in any one glossary.

Campaign

One investigation, scoped to one subject. It owns a glossary, a plan, a roadmap, and its working material. Avoid: study, project, investigation as a proper noun.

Subject

The category a campaign is ultimately about — never a particular artefact. A finding matters only insofar as it generalises to the subject.

Specimen

The particular artefact being dissected to learn about the subject. Chosen for being open and readable rather than for being representative.

Transfer specimen

A second artefact the vocabulary was not derived from, used to falsify it. Predictions are committed before it is examined, or the test proves nothing.
